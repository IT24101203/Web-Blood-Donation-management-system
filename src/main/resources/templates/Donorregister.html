Donor Eligibility Requirements

  • • You must be between 18-65 years old to donate blood
  • • You must weigh at least 50kg (110 lbs)
  • • You must be in good health and feeling well
  • • You can donate whole blood every 8 weeks (56 days)
  • • Please bring a valid photo ID for verification
  • • Your NIC will serve as your unique donor identification
